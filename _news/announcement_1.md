Congratulations to our PhD student Haochen Zhou on presenting his research at <a href="https://utsg.net/annual-conference_2026">the Universities' Transport Study Group (UTSG) Annual Conference 2026</a>, hosted by the University of Surrey from 13 to 15 July 2026.

His research, entitled "Multi-objective Optimal Energy Management of Hybrid Energy Storage Systems for Electric Vehicles", develops a model predictive control (MPC) strategy to optimise battery-supercapacitor power sharing, reduce battery degradation, and improve energy management.

The results demonstrate a 24.43% reduction in simulated battery state-of-health loss compared with a battery-only system under the WLTC driving cycle, highlighting the potential for more durable electric vehicle energy storage systems.

The research was conducted in collaboration with the <a href="https://commission.europa.eu/about/departments-and-executive-agencies/joint-research-centre_en">European Commission's Joint Research Centre (JRC)</a>.

Authors: Haochen Zhou, Alessandro Tansini, Georgios Fontaras, and Yinglong He.
