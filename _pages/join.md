## Join the EASy Research Group

We welcome motivated students and researchers interested in **electrification, automation, intelligent control, and optimisation**.

Our research spans electric and autonomous vehicles, robotics, electric aviation, space systems, and maritime applications.

We offer opportunities for PhD research, postdoctoral collaboration, undergraduate and Master's projects, and academic visits.
